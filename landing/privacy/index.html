SokeriPilot

Privacy

Your readings stay with you.

SokeriPilot has no account, no server of its own and no analytics. This page describes exactly what the app reads, what it keeps and the two optional paths where data leaves the phone. Last updated 25 November 2026, for version 1.2.1.

What the app reads

  • The glucose value, trend, timestamp and source app name from notifications posted by the CGM app you already use, after you grant Android's notification access once.
  • Optionally, an xDrip+ local broadcast when xDrip+ is installed and its local broadcast is enabled.
  • Optionally, a Nightscout site you configure: the entries endpoint at the URL you enter.
  • The sensor's own state (warm-up, signal loss, sensor end, battery) when the CGM app states it.

What stays on the phone

  • Your target range, unit preference, driving-mode switch and the optional Nightscout URL.
  • The Nightscout key as the SHA-1 digest the protocol requires — the raw key is never written to disk.
  • Four numbers from the last drive: minutes, time in range, lowest value and low count.
  • The source-check list of the last ten CGM-looking notifications, in memory only; it disappears when the process ends and is never written or logged.

Settings live in the app's private storage and are excluded from Android cloud backup, device transfer and adb backup. There is no glucose history database: readings are shown as they arrive and are not archived by SokeriPilot.

What can leave the phone

  • Your Nightscout server — only if you enter its URL and key. Requests go to that host over HTTPS, with redirects rejected.
  • Health Connect — only if you turn the export on and grant the permission. New readings are written as blood glucose records; the app never reads health data back, and revoking the permission stops the export.
  • A settings file — only when you choose Export. It contains your URL, range and units, in a file you pick the location of.

Nothing else is transmitted. There is no crash reporting, advertising, attribution or third-party SDK, and the Android manifest declares no tracking permission.

This website

The site is static HTML, CSS and one JavaScript file served by Cloudflare Workers. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from another origin: every image, font and script comes from the same host, and the Content-Security-Policy blocks outbound connections entirely. Downloading an APK is a plain file request; no form, account or email is involved.

Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the request metadata needed to serve and protect the site (IP address, user agent, requested path) under its own terms. The project does not receive or store that metadata.

Removing your data

Uninstalling SokeriPilot deletes its settings and the last-drive numbers. Nightscout history lives on your own server and is removed there. Health Connect records can be deleted in the Health Connect app at any time; revoking SokeriPilot's permission stops new writes immediately.

Medical scope

SokeriPilot is not a medical device. It is a viewing and reminder tool, does not recommend treatment or insulin doses, and never replaces your sensor's official app or your healthcare professional's guidance. Alerts are advisory.
